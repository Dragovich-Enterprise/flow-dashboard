How money is counted

Count money from outside as income
Zelle, Venmo, and transfers from other banks count as income. Moves between your own linked accounts never do.

Returns and credits are subtracted from the category they came from. Always on.

Import older history

Capital One only shares ~90 days through Plaid. Upload monthly statement PDFs (or a CSV) from your bank, pick the account, and check the list before it's saved. You can choose several statements at once. Only transactions older than what your bank already synced are added, so nothing is counted twice.
