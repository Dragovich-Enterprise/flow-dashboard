How money is counted

Count money from outside as income
Zelle, Venmo, and transfers from other banks count as income. Moves between your own linked accounts never do.
Refunds reduce spending
Returns and credits are subtracted from the category they came from. Always on.

Import older history (CSV)

Capital One only shares ~90 days through Plaid. Download older transactions as CSV from your bank's website, then import them here. Only rows older than the earliest synced transaction for that account are added, so nothing is double-counted.
